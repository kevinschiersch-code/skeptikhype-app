Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This tool ("the App") is a personal, non-commercial application built and operated by a single individual for the sole purpose of managing and analyzing their own "Skeptic Hype" TikTok account. It has no other users, does not collect data from any third party, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

What data is accessed

Via the TikTok API, and only after the account owner's own explicit OAuth authorization, the App reads that account's own public profile statistics (e.g. follower count) and own video performance metrics (views, likes, comments, shares), and, where authorized, publishes video content to that same account.

Storage

Any data retrieved is stored locally on the account owner's own device/environment for personal analysis. It is not transmitted to or stored on any third-party server, and not shared with anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account owner via the "Skeptic Hype" TikTok account.
